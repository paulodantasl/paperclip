## Document Management Strategy (Paperclip Integration)

### Document Hierarchy
1. **Master Documents** - Core policies and procedures
2. **Template Documents** - Reusable templates for operations
3. **Project Documents** - Project-specific docs
4. **Compliance Documents** - Regulatory and audit trails
5. **Historical Documents** - Archive of completed projects

### Version Control & Updates
- All documents will use Paperclip's versioning
- Quarterly review of all master documents
- Change tracking for compliance
- Access control by role

### Document Access Levels
- **Public**: Company policies, handbooks
- **Internal**: Operational procedures, templates
- **Confidential**: Financial, strategic plans
- **Restricted**: Personnel, compliance sensitive

## Risk Management

### Key Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Compliance violations | High | Regular audits, dedicated compliance role |
| Safety incidents | High | Comprehensive safety program, training |
| Cash flow issues | High | Strict financial controls, payment terms |
| Skilled labor shortage | Medium | Competitive compensation, training program |
| Project cost overruns | Medium | Strict estimation, change control process |
| Customer disputes | Medium | Clear contracts, communication protocol |
